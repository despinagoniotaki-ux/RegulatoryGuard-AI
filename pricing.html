The Preclinical Regulatory Bottleneck

Unmanaged Data Anomalies Trigger RTF Letters

Moving a therapeutic candidate from preclinical testing to Phase 1 trials requires absolute data consistency across millions of toxicology and pharmacology records. Minor everyday lab shifts—such as a 4% volume error or titration variance—get buried in spreadsheets. When compiled for FDA IND submissions, these undocumented deltas stand out, triggering costly delays.

  • Prohibitive licensing fees from legacy enterprise platforms lock out lean labs.
  • Manual spreadsheet audits lead to human transcription and formatting errors.
The RegulatoryGuard AI Advantage
